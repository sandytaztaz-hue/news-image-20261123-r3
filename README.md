A 1024x1024 broadsheet front page summing up Monday 23 November 2026 in
one image: 36 verified stories across 7 regions, 13 original flat-colour
drawings, white paper with an ink masthead, a navy timeline band, and a
dotted-halftone world map on navy with large red numbered pins at true
positions, plus a DATE CHECK box that names the stories that did not happen
on Monday and says when they did.

## What is on the page

- Masthead: "Monday 23 November 2026", LATE EDITION, press time about
  16:00 UTC, Monday 23 November 2026.
- Stat strip with the six Monday numbers: 429 / −5.8% / 7 / 20+ / 10 / 239/5.
- Regions: Europe 10 · Middle East 7 · Asia 5 · Americas 8 · Africa 2 ·
  Oceania 2 · Global 2 (36 stories in total).
- The 3 Monday heroes: COP31 aftermath in Antalya, Zelenskyy on the sunk
  Merkury off Benghazi, Belgium shuts its skies in Brussels.
- The 10 minis with their cities: Dymer, Gulf of Finland, Warsaw,
  Wellington, Istanbul, Paris, Herzliya, Seoul, Liverpool (Sunday),
  Washington (Sunday). Minis 12 and 13 are Sunday stories, and their
  kickers say SUNDAY in red — the page never presents them as Monday news.
- The map: dotted-halftone continents on a navy sea, red numbered pins
  1–13; stories 35 and 36 have no one place, so no pin.
- ALSO ON 14–36, DATE CHECK, and HOW WE CHECKED.

## Run it

MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png` and verifies its SHA-256
against the pinned hash; it exits non-zero if the copy does not match.
